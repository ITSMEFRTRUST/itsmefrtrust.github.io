Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

itsme is a private, single-user tool. It does not collect, sell or share personal data from visitors or other users, and it has no accounts other than the owner's own TikTok account.

  • What is stored: only the owner's TikTok access token, kept on the owner's own private server so the tool can post the owner's videos.
  • What is not stored: no data about other TikTok users, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no cookies on this site.
  • Sharing: data is not shared with any third party. It is only sent to TikTok to publish the owner's own videos.
  • Deletion: the owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in TikTok settings, which invalidates the stored token, and can delete it from the server.
